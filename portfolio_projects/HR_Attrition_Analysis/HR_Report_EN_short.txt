HR ATTRITION ANALYSIS - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (EN)
==================================================

Dataset: 1,470 employees, 16.1% attrition rate.

Key Findings:
  . Overtime is strongly associated with attrition. Bayesian A/B test: overtime employees have 10.4% attrition vs 30.5% for non-overtime.
  . Log-rank test p=0.0000: significant survival difference by overtime status.
  . Best model: Logistic Regression (L1) (AUC-ROC=0.805, AUC-PR=0.579).
  . Optimal decision threshold: 0.05 (minimizes cost at $-1,230,000).
  . Top drivers: Overtime, Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance, tenure.

Recommendations:
  1. Review overtime policies and their impact on retention.
  2. Improve work-life balance policies and environment satisfaction.
  3. Target retention programs at early-tenure employees.
  4. Monitor compensation equity across roles.
